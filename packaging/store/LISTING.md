**Product name:** Nebulance

**Description** [10000]:

> Year 2097. The NEBULANCE probe came back from a distant comet carrying cosmic dust... and a signal.
> The signal called the Swarm: millions of star insects in attack formation. The fleet fell in hours.
> What's left: a hidden hangar, one ship and you.
>
> Nebulance is a formation shoot 'em up made in the spirit of the shareware games we used to play on
> the family PC. Your ship only moves sideways, every tap of the fire button is a burst, and the rare
> coins you collect buy stronger and stronger weapons between sectors.
>
> • A 100-level campaign in 20 sectors, with checkpoints. Every level is different: its own formation,
> movement, entry paths, featured enemy, diving style and salvos.
> • A shop that sells everything, from cheap upgrades to the most powerful weapons: Fireball, Plasma,
> Railgun, Meteor and more, each with its own sound.
> • Weapon evolution: max out a weapon and the shop offers to evolve it. The Fireball becomes a mini
> sun, the Railgun fires twin beams...
> • Boss fights that change the stage: darkness, rising ground, meteor showers.
> • Elite enemies, bonus stages and rift minigames.
> • Local co-op for two players: two gamepads, keyboard and gamepad, or both on one keyboard.
> • Ships to unlock, collectible cards, achievements, ranks and trails.
> • Full gamepad support (Xbox, PlayStation and generic), mouse menus and fully remappable controls.
> • An original synthesized chiptune soundtrack, with a theme for every region.
> • Sharp pixel art at any resolution, 4K included.
> • English and Brazilian Portuguese.
>
> No ads, no microtransactions, no internet connection needed.
